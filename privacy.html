Beamshow privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

The short version

Beamshow does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It does not use the network at all. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app does with data

  • Songs. A song you add is copied into the app and converted on your device. The beat analysis and the light show are computed on your device too. Nothing is uploaded.
  • USB drives. When you send a show, the app writes two files into a LightShow folder on the drive you chose and reads them back to check them. It never changes other files on the drive. It remembers which drive you chose, on your device, so you do not have to pick it again.
  • Purchase. Beamshow is a paid app. The payment is handled entirely by Apple. Beamshow never sees your payment details or identity.

Data retention and deletion

Everything Beamshow keeps is on your device. Delete a show in the app to remove it and its song, or delete the app to remove all of it. Because nothing is sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Third parties

Beamshow contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and shares nothing with third parties.

Children

Beamshow collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Awis13/beamshow-support/issues.
