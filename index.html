Beamshow

Make a custom light show for your car from any song, edit it on a timeline and put it on a USB drive.

Support

Questions, bug reports or a show that does not play in the car: open an issue at github.com/Awis13/beamshow-support/issues. Please include your device, the app version, the car model and its software version.

Getting a show into the car

  • Use a USB drive formatted as exFAT or FAT32 (ext3 and ext4 work too). NTFS, APFS and Mac OS Extended do not.
  • Do not use the dashcam drive: a drive with a TeslaCam folder is not offered for Light Show.
  • In the app, choose Send to Drive and pick the drive itself, not a folder on it. The app writes a LightShow folder at the top of the drive and reads every file back to check it.
  • On a Mac, the first time you use a drive the app asks you to open it once in a file dialog, so it may write to it.
  • In the car: park, plug the drive into a front USB port, open Toybox, then Light Show, and tap Schedule Show.
